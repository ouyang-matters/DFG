Full description (max 4000, currently 1919)
I have pretty bad OCD, so my brain loves turning tiny things into full investigations. Did I turn off the stove? Did I close the garage door? Did I lock the thing I definitely remember locking five minutes ago?

I used to take photos of everything just so Future Me would stop freaking out. That worked, except my camera roll became 40% appliances, doors and switches. And I am way too busy to organise a pile of photos I will probably never look at.

So I made this. A simple place to check things off and keep proof when I need it, without turning my photo gallery into an evidence archive for my own brain.

HOW IT WORKS

Add a check, then say when it should happen. A specific date, certain days of the week, certain days of the month, or every so many minutes, hours, days, weeks or months.

Each schedule gets a time window. One entry has to land inside that window. If the window closes with nothing logged, you get a reminder. Checking early does not count for a window that has not opened yet, which is the whole point.

TWO WAYS TO LOG

Take a photo when you want proof, or just tap when you do not. Notes are optional either way. You choose which kinds a check accepts, and you can change your mind later without losing anything you already logged.

PHOTOS THAT STAY OUT OF YOUR WAY

Every photo lives inside the app, filed under the check it belongs to, never mixed into your camera roll. Browse them as a grid, select several at once, and delete the entry and the file together.

A CLEAR TIMELINE

Every entry keeps its time, its note and its photo, grouped by day, so you can look back and see exactly when something was done.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

No account, no sign in, no internet permission at all. Nothing leaves your phone. Nothing is uploaded, tracked or shared, because the app cannot reach the network in the first place.

Available in English, French, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
