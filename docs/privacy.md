# Privacy Policy for Rounds

Last updated: 8 September 2026

## The short version

Rounds does not collect anything. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, and
no internet permission at all. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

Rounds keeps the following on your phone only:

- The checks you create, with their names, notes and schedules.
- The entries you log, with their times and any notes you add.
- The photos you take or pick for a check.

All of it lives in the app's private storage. Photos are kept inside the app,
under the check they belong to, and are not added to your camera roll or shared
media.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it is not
technically able to send your data anywhere, to us or to anyone else. There is
no server, no account and no sync.

## Permissions and why they exist

- **Notifications** are used to remind you when a check window closes with
  nothing logged. Nothing else is ever posted.
- **Camera**: Rounds does not hold the camera permission. Taking a photo hands
  the job to your own camera app, which returns the picture to Rounds.
- **Boot completed** is used to restore your pending reminders after a restart,
  because Android clears scheduled alarms when the device reboots.

## Backup

If you have Android backup enabled, your device may include the app's data in
the backup it makes to your own Google account. That backup is between you,
your device and Google. The developer of Rounds has no access to it. You can
disable it in your device's backup settings.

## Deleting your data

Deleting an entry removes its photo file too. Deleting a check removes all of
its entries and photos. Uninstalling Rounds removes everything it stored.

## Children

Rounds is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same
address as this document, with a new date at the top.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact
address listed on the app's Google Play listing.
